# SKILLSWAP LOCAL - PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 2025

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

SkillSwap Local ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the SkillSwap Local mobile application (the "App").

**Please read this policy carefully.** If you do not agree with our privacy practices, please do not use the App.

---

## 2. DEFINITIONS

- **Personal Information:** Any data that identifies you, including your name, email, ID number, photograph, location, or payment information.
- **Sensitive Data:** Government-issued ID numbers, biometric data (selfies), financial information, and health-related details.
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on data, including collection, storage, use, transfer, or deletion.
- **Data Controller:** SkillSwap Local, which determines how and why data is processed.
- **Data Processor:** Third-party services (Firebase, Cloudinary) that process data on our behalf.

---

## 3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 3.1 Information You Provide Directly

#### During Registration:
- **Full Legal Name** (required)
- **Email Address** (required; currently limited to Gmail)
- **Password** (hashed and encrypted; we never store plain text passwords)
- **Phone Number** (optional)
- **Government-Issued ID** (required; uploaded for verification)
- **Selfie/Photo ID** (required; uploaded for verification and optionally as profile picture)

#### During Profile Completion:
- **Bio / Personal Description** (optional)
- **City / Barangay / Location** (required)
- **Skills Offered** (optional; comma-separated list)
- **Skill Description** (optional; detailed explanation of services)
- **Availability** (day/time preferences)
- **Exchange Modes** (Skill Swap / Cash)
- **Price Range** (if accepting cash; providers set their own rates)
- **Profile Picture** (optional; may be auto-filled from submitted selfie)

#### During Transactions:
- **Booking Requests:** Service needed, preferred date/time, meeting location, notes
- **Proof of Work:** Photo uploaded after session completion
- **Ratings & Reviews:** Star rating (1-5), review text, optional tags
- **Messages & Chats:** Text content, images, videos shared in conversations
- **Reports:** Details of violations or disputes reported to admin

#### Optional Information:
- **Bank Account Details** (if provided for future payment features)
- **Additional Photos or Certificates** (if shared in chat or profile)

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect:
- **Login Activity:** Timestamps of app access and logout
- **Features Used:** Which screens/functions you access
- **Device Information:** Device model, OS version, unique device identifier
- **Usage Patterns:** How often you log in, which features you use
- **App Crashes & Errors:** Technical errors for debugging and improvement
- **Location Data:** Your approximate city/barangay (not precise GPS tracking, current version)
- **IP Address:** For security and fraud detection

### 3.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive data from:
- **Firebase Authentication:** Login records and email verification status
- **Cloudinary:** File upload logs and media delivery analytics
- **Other Users:** Information they provide about you (in reviews, reports, or conversations)
- **Law Enforcement:** If required by legal process

### 3.4 Cookies & Tracking

The App does **not** currently use cookies. However, third-party services (Firebase, Cloudinary) may use cookies or similar tracking technologies. See their privacy policies for details.

## 5. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

### 5.1 Information Shared with Other Users

When your account is marked as "discoverable," your profile is visible to other users. Shared information includes:
- **Public Profile:** Name, bio, skills offered, availability, location (city/barangay level)
- **Ratings & Reviews:** Your average rating and reviews posted by clients
- **Chat Messages:** Messages you send to other users
- **Your Booking History:** Other users can see when you've completed sessions (as a trust signal)

**You can hide your profile at any time** by toggling "Show my profile in Nearby Providers" OFF.

### 5.2 Information Shared with Admins

Administrators (employed by SkillSwap Local) have access to:
- **All user data** (names, IDs, selfies, emails, locations, phone numbers)
- **All messages and chats** (to investigate disputes and violations)
- **All booking records** (to monitor transactions and resolve conflicts)
- **All reports and flags** (to investigate user violations)
- **Payments data** (current version: peer-to-peer; future: in-app processing)

Admins are bound by confidentiality agreements and use data only for moderation and platform management.

### 5.3 Information Shared with Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers (Data Processors), who process your data on our behalf:

#### Firebase (Google Cloud)
- **Purpose:** User authentication, database storage (Firestore), backup
- **Data Processed:** Email, password hash, profile data, booking records, messages
- **Location:** Google servers (US, EU, Asia-Pacific)
- **Privacy Policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy

#### Cloudinary
- **Purpose:** Image and video storage (IDs, selfies, profile pics, chat media, proof of work)
- **Data Processed:** Photos, videos, metadata
- **Location:** Cloudinary servers (US, EU)
- **Privacy Policy:** https://cloudinary.com/privacy

#### OpenStreetMap / Nominatim
- **Purpose:** Location search and geocoding for booking location picker
- **Data Processed:** Location searches (not stored; real-time only)
- **Location:** Open-source infrastructure (EU)
- **Privacy Policy:** https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

### 5.4 Legal Disclosure

We may disclose your information without consent if:
- Required by law or legal process (court order, subpoena, government request)
- Necessary to protect the safety or rights of users or the Company
- In response to a data breach or security threat
- Needed to enforce our Terms of Service

## 6. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

### 6.1 Retention Schedule

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active Account Data** | For the duration of your account | Service provision |
| **Government ID & Selfie** | 2 years after account deletion | Legal compliance & fraud prevention |

| **Failed Login Attempts** | 30 days | Security |
| **Support Tickets** | 2 years | Customer service |

### 6.2 Deletion Options

**You can delete your account at any time:**
1. Go to **Profile Settings**
2. Tap **Delete Account**
3. Confirm your decision (irreversible)
4. Your profile is deactivated immediately
5. Personal data is deleted within 30 days

**What remains after deletion:**
- Your public reviews and ratings (attributed to "Deleted User")
- Booking records (for other users' reference)
- Reports you submitted (under your name)

### 6.3 Right to Be Forgotten

Under GDPR (if applicable), you have the right to request deletion of your data. However:
- We may retain data as required by Philippine law
- Public reviews and ratings may remain
- Booking records are retained for legal/tax purposes
- We will comply with all applicable laws

**To request deletion, email:** privacy@skillswaplocal.com

## 7. SECURITY & PROTECTION

### 7.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:
- **Encryption in Transit:** SSL/TLS encryption for all data sent to/from the App
- **Encryption at Rest:** Sensitive data (IDs, passwords) encrypted in storage
- **Password Security:** Passwords hashed using bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
- **Access Controls:** Admin access restricted to verified employees
- **Firewalls & Intrusion Detection:** Cloudflare & Firebase security
- **Regular Security Audits:** Periodic testing and vulnerability assessments
- **Secure Deletion:** Deleted data overwritten to prevent recovery

### 7.2 What We Cannot Guarantee

Despite our efforts, **no security system is 100% secure**:
- Hackers may breach our infrastructure (we will notify you immediately)
- Network interception may occur (mitigated by SSL/TLS)
- Insider threats are possible (mitigated by access controls & monitoring)
- Your device may be compromised (protect your password & don't share your phone)

### 7.3 Your Responsibility

You are responsible for:
- Keeping your password confidential
- Logging out on shared devices
- Reporting suspicious activity immediately
- Protecting your smartphone from unauthorized access
- Not sharing sensitive information outside the App

## 8. YOUR RIGHTS & CHOICES

### 8.1 Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA-Compliant)

If you are located in the EU, California, or other privacy-forward jurisdictions, you have the right to:

#### Right to Access
- You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
- **Request process:** Email privacy@skillswaplocal.com with subject "Data Access Request"
- **Response time:** Within 30 days

#### Right to Correction
- You can update your profile information anytime in the App
- For sensitive data (ID, selfie), contact admin to request correction
- Incorrect data must be updated within 30 days

#### Right to Deletion ("Right to Be Forgotten")
- You can request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Exceptions:** We may retain data required by law or for legitimate business purposes
- **Request process:** Email privacy@skillswaplocal.com with subject "Deletion Request"

#### Right to Restrict Processing
- You can request that we limit how we use your data
- **Example:** Not using your data for analytics
- Processing is restricted pending our response

#### Right to Data Portability
- You can request your data in a portable format (e.g., CSV, JSON)
- **Request process:** Email privacy@skillswaplocal.com with subject "Data Portability Request"
- **Response time:** Within 30 days

#### Right to Object
- You can object to processing of your data for marketing or analytics
- **How to object:** Email privacy@skillswaplocal.com
- We will stop processing unless we have compelling legal grounds

#### Right to Non-Discrimination
- We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights
- You may use the App without providing unnecessary personal data

### 8.2 Marketing Preferences

To manage communications:
- **Opt-out of promotional emails:** Unsubscribe link in every email
- **Disable push notifications:** Settings > Notifications > Off (when feature launches)
- **Request not to be contacted:** Email privacy@skillswaplocal.com

### 8.3 Cookie Preferences

The App does not currently use cookies, but third-party services may. To manage:
