# SKILLSWAP LOCAL - COMMUNITY GUIDELINES & CODE OF CONDUCT

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 2025

---

## PREAMBLE

SkillSwap Local is built on the foundation of **trust, respect, and mutual benefit**. Our community consists of skilled individuals eager to learn from one another, exchange services fairly, and build meaningful local connections.

These Community Guidelines define the standards for behavior on the platform. By using SkillSwap Local, you commit to upholding these standards and help us create a safe, inclusive, and productive environment for everyone.

**Our Commitment:** We moderate fairly, transparently, and consistently. Violations result in proportionate consequences, with opportunity for appeal.

---

## 1. RESPECT & INCLUSION

### 1.1 Treat All Users with Respect
- Address others professionally and courteously
- Respect differences in background, culture, language, and identity
- Do not engage in personal attacks, insults, or mockery
- Acknowledge others' perspectives, even if you disagree

### 1.2 Zero Tolerance for Harassment & Discrimination

**We do NOT tolerate:**
- Hate speech based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality
- Sexual harassment or unwanted advances
- Threats of violence or intimidation
- Doxxing (sharing personal contact information without consent)
- Cyberbullying or coordinated attacks
- Discrimination in service provision (e.g., refusing to work with someone based on protected characteristics)

**Consequences:**
- **First offense:** Warning + mandatory sensitivity training (future feature)
- **Second offense:** 7-day account suspension + mandatory training
- **Third offense:** Permanent account termination

### 1.3 Inclusive Language
- Use inclusive and non-gendered language when possible
- Respect pronouns and names chosen by users
- Avoid slurs, stereotypes, or dehumanizing language
- Correct yourself and move forward if you misspeak

## 2. HONESTY & AUTHENTICITY

### 2.1 Profile Truthfulness
Your profile must be:
- **Accurate:** All information is current and correct
- **Honest:** No exaggerations or false claims about skills
- **Complete:** All required fields are filled truthfully
- **Authentic:** The profile photo is of you (not a celebrity or AI-generated image)

**What we verify:**
- Government-issued ID matches your name
- Selfie matches the ID photo
- Name and basic info are truthful
- Skills offered are realistic and your own

**Prohibited:**
- Using someone else's photos
- Claiming fake certifications or credentials
- Fake account names or aliases
- AI-generated or heavily edited ID photos

### 2.2 Accurate Service Descriptions
When offering a skill, describe what clients will actually receive:
- Be honest about your experience level (beginner, intermediate, expert)
- Specify what's included and what's not
- Disclose any limitations (e.g., "I only offer online tutoring")
- Update your profile if your skills or availability change
- Don't oversell or under-deliver

### 2.3 Booking Integrity
When requesting a booking:
- Honestly describe what you need
- Be clear about your expectations
- Disclose any special requirements upfront
- Don't misrepresent your needs to secure lower rates
- Follow through on committed skill swaps

## 3. SAFETY & SECURITY

### 3.1 Physical Safety Precautions
- Meet in public, well-populated locations
- Tell a trusted friend where you're going and when you expect to return
- Trust your instincts; cancel sessions that feel unsafe
- Report unsafe user behavior to admins immediately
- Don't share your home address with new contacts; meet at a neutral location first

### 3.2 Protecting Personal Information
**DO NOT share:**
- Your full home address in the app (only city/barangay)
- Phone number outside the app (use the app's chat)
- Financial information (bank account, credit card details)
- Government ID numbers (you've already verified during registration)
- Social media profiles unless you're comfortable with public connection

**DO:**
- Use the app's chat feature for all communication
- Verify identities before meeting
- Take screenshots of agreements (in case of disputes)
- Meet in person before agreeing to large transactions

### 3.3 Preventing Scams & Fraud
**Common scams to watch for:**
- Requests for upfront payment (especially for skill swaps)
- Pressure to pay outside the app
- Requests to connect via WhatsApp or Telegram before meeting
- Too-good-to-be-true deals or rates
- Requests for personal information disguised as "verification"

**If you suspect fraud:**
- Report the user immediately
- Do not send money or exchange services
- Share evidence with admins
- Consider blocking the user

### 3.4 Safe Use Practices
- Keep your account password secure; don't share it
- Log out on public/shared devices
- Enable two-factor authentication (when available)
- Verify you're communicating with the real person (not an impostor)
- Save important messages in case you need them for disputes

- Drugs, weapons, explosives, or controlled substances
- Adult/sexual services or exploitation
- Human trafficking or slavery-related services
- Stolen goods or services
- Money laundering or unauthorized financial activities
- Hacking, malware, or cybercrime
- Counterfeit documents or goods
- Any activity that violates Philippine law

**Consequences:** Immediate account termination + referral to law enforcement

### 4.2 Sexual, Explicit & Adult Content
**Forbidden:**
- Nude or sexually suggestive photos/videos
- Sexual solicitation or "dating" services
- Explicit sexual language or harassment
- Child exploitation material (zero tolerance; reported to authorities)
- Proposals for escorts or paid intimate services

**Consequences:** Immediate permanent termination + potential law enforcement referral

### 4.3 Hateful, Violent & Threatening Content
**Forbidden:**
- Violent threats or incitement to violence
- Content promoting terrorism or extremism
- Hate speech, slurs, or dehumanizing language
- Doxxing (sharing private information to harm)
- Calls for violence against groups
- Content promoting self-harm or suicide

**Consequences:**
- Immediate account suspension pending investigation
- Mandatory reporting to law enforcement if threats are credible
- Permanent termination if substantiated

### 4.4 Deceptive Practices
**Forbidden:**
- Impersonating others (including admins or verified users)
- Fake reviews or artificially inflating ratings
- Phishing, fake links, or social engineering
- Manipulation of the booking system
- Gaming the rating system with fake accounts
- Bait-and-switch tactics (advertise one service, deliver another)

**Consequences:** Account suspension, review deletion, rating reset, possible permanent termination

### 4.5 Spam & Commercial Exploitation
**Forbidden:**
- Unsolicited bulk messages or "spam"
- Promoting external businesses or products
- Multi-level marketing (MLM) or pyramid schemes
- Selling email addresses or contact info
- Recruiting for competing platforms
- Excessive self-promotion in messages or profiles

**Consequences:** Muting, suspension, or permanent termination

### 4.6 Intellectual Property Violations
**Forbidden:**
- Posting others' copyrighted content without permission
- Sharing pirated software, movies, or music
- Reproducing tutorials or courses without attribution
- Teaching skills that require licensing (e.g., professional certifications) without credentials
- Using others' logos or trademarked content

**Consequences:** Content removal, warning, suspension, legal action if applicable

## 5. BOOKING CONDUCT & FULFILLMENT

### 5.1 Honesty in Bookings
**Providers must:**
- Accurately describe the service
- Clarify duration, location, and deliverables
- Disclose any limitations (e.g., "online only," "minimum 2-hour session")
- Confirm availability before accepting bookings
- Not double-book or overbill

**Clients must:**
- Be honest about their needs and abilities
- Provide clear instructions or context
- Confirm the date and time before the session
- Show up on time (or notify the provider if you're running late)
- Be respectful during the session

### 5.2 Professional Conduct During Sessions
**Providers must:**
- Arrive on time (or notify the client of delays)
- Provide the agreed-upon service in good faith
- Be professional and courteous
- Respect the client's time and space
- Not deviate from the agreed service without permission

**Clients must:**
- Be punctual and ready to receive the service
- Provide a safe and appropriate environment
- Be respectful and cooperative
- Not demand services outside the agreement
- Provide honest feedback after completion

### 5.3 Completion & Proof of Work
**Providers must:**
- Upload a genuine photo of completed work (not staged)
- Include identifying details (date, location, in-progress shots)
- Not fake or manipulate proof photos
- Mark the booking as complete only after the service is finished
- Accept client disputes if proof is insufficient

**Clients must:**
- Accept completion if the service meets expectations
- Provide fair, honest ratings and reviews
- Not dispute completion to avoid paying or swapping
- Request amendments before disputing (if applicable)

### 5.4 Communication Standards
**Both parties must:**
- Respond to messages within 24 hours (reasonable effort)
- Be professional and clear in conversations
- Not pressure or harass the other party
- Resolve disagreements calmly and directly
- Escalate to admins only if direct resolution fails
- Not contact the other party outside the app to bypass moderation

## 6. RATING & REVIEW INTEGRITY

### 6.1 Fair Rating Practices
**Clients must rate fairly:**
- Rate based on the actual service provided, not personal preference
- Consider the provider's effort and professionalism
- Give constructive feedback, not punishment
- Not rate out of anger or spite
- Not collude with others to manipulate ratings

**Providers must accept ratings gracefully:**
- Not demand changes or removal of negative reviews
- Not retaliate with negative reviews
- Respond to criticism professionally
- Learn from feedback and improve
- Request admin review only if the review is clearly false/defamatory

### 6.2 Review Content Standards
**Acceptable reviews:**
- Honest feedback on the service quality
- Constructive criticism with specific examples
- Praise for exceptional service
- Helpful details for future clients

**Unacceptable reviews:**
- Hateful, harassing, or defamatory language
- False claims or fabricated stories
- Personal attacks unrelated to the service
- Spam or promotional content
- Threats or demands

### 6.3 One Review Per Booking
- You may rate a provider only once per completed booking
- You cannot rate or review the same provider multiple times for the same session
- Ratings cannot be edited after submission
- Only admins can remove reviews (for violation of guidelines)

## 7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION & ESCALATION

### 7.1 Direct Resolution (First Step)
**If a problem occurs:**
1. **Communicate:** Message the other party within the app chat
2. **Explain:** Be specific about the issue and what you need
3. **Negotiate:** Propose solutions (rescheduling, partial refund, alternate service)
4. **Document:** Save screenshots of messages and agreements

**Most disputes are resolved at this stage. This is always the first step.**

### 7.2 Admin Mediation (Second Step)
If direct communication fails, escalate to admins:
1. **Submit a Report:** Use the "Report User" or "Report Issue" feature
2. **Provide Evidence:** Share screenshots, messages, booking details
3. **Explain:** Clearly describe the problem and what resolution you seek
4. **Wait:** Admins respond within 5-7 business days

**Admins will:**
- Review all evidence from both parties
- Contact both users for additional context if needed
- Determine what happened based on the Terms & Guidelines
- Suggest a resolution (rescheduling, apology, account warning, etc.)
- Document the decision

### 7.3 Possible Outcomes
**Admin decisions may include:**
- **No violation found:** Case dismissed
- **Both partially at fault:** Both receive warnings; mutual apology requested
- **One party at fault:** Responsible party warned or suspended; remediation required
- **Service quality dispute:** Booking marked as disputed; rating may be adjusted
- **Payment dispute:** User advised to pursue legal remedies (admins do not process refunds for peer-to-peer transactions)

### 7.4 What Admins Cannot Do
Admins **cannot:**
- Process refunds (app does not hold escrow in current version)
- Force payment from non-paying users
- Override ratings or delete reviews (except for rule violations)
- Provide legal advice
- Act as judges in civil disputes
- Investigate claims requiring law enforcement

## 8. ENFORCEMENT & CONSEQUENCES

### 8.1 Warning System

| Violation Type | Severity | First Offense | Second Offense | Third Offense |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mild Rudeness** | Minor | Warning | 3-day suspension | 7-day suspension |
| **Spam or Mild Harassment** | Minor | Warning | 7-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| **No-Show** | Medium | Warning | 7-day suspension | Account suspension (3-strike system) |
| **Fake Profile/ID** | High | 7-day suspension | 30-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| **Explicit Content** | High | 7-day suspension | 30-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| **Hate Speech/Threats** | Critical | 30-day suspension | Permanent ban | N/A |
| **Illegal Activity/Exploitation** | Critical | Permanent ban + law enforcement referral | N/A | N/A |

### 8.2 Account Suspension
**What happens during suspension:**
- You cannot log in to the App
- Your profile is hidden from search
- Active bookings are cancelled
- Messages cannot be sent or received
- No new bookings can be made

**Suspension duration:**
- **3-day suspension:** Minor violations
- **7-day suspension:** Repeated minor violations
- **30-day suspension:** Serious violations
- **Permanent ban:** Critical violations (illegal activity, exploitation, repeated harassment)

### 8.3 Permanent Account Termination
Your account will be **permanently deleted and you cannot re-register** if you:
- Engage in illegal activities
- Exploit, harass, or threaten users
- Provide fake identity documents
- Violate the app terms after multiple warnings
- Commit fraud or scams
- Post child exploitation material
- Engage in sexual exploitation or services

**Permanent termination is NOT appealable.**

### 8.4 Appeal Process
If you believe your account was suspended unfairly:
1. Email **appeals@skillswaplocal.com** within 7 days
2. Explain why you believe the decision was wrong
3. Provide evidence or context admins may have missed
4. Appeals are reviewed by a senior admin (not the original reviewer)
5. You will receive a response within 10 business days

**Notes on appeals:**
- Only suspensions can be appealed (permanent bans are final)
- Appeals are reviewed fairly but admissions of guilt strengthen the case against you
- If the appeal is denied, you cannot appeal again

## 9. CONTENT MODERATION PROCESS

### 9.1 How We Find Violations
We monitor the platform through:
- **User Reports:** Other users report violations using the "Report User" feature
- **Admin Review:** Admins manually review flagged accounts and interactions
- **Automated Systems:** Filters for keywords, patterns, and known scam signals (future feature)
- **Law Enforcement Referrals:** Reports from authorities

### 9.2 Investigation Process
1. **Report Received:** User or system flags a violation
2. **Initial Review:** Admin reviews the report and gathers evidence
3. **Context Gathering:** Admin may contact both parties for additional context
4. **Decision Making:** Admin determines if a violation occurred
5. **Action Taken:** Account is warned, suspended, or terminated
6. **Documentation:** Decision is logged for audit purposes

### 9.3 Transparency & Appeals
- Users have the right to know why they were actioned
- We provide specific reasons for suspensions
- Users can appeal within 7 days
- Appeals are reviewed by a different admin than the original reviewer
- Decisions are final after appeal (except where law requires otherwise)

## 12. SPECIAL RULES FOR DIFFERENT EXCHANGE MODES

### 12.1 Skill Swaps (Barter)
**Standard practice:**
- Both parties agree to exchange services of roughly equal value
- No money changes hands
- Both parties complete their services in good faith
- Disputes are resolved through the mediation process

**Fair swap practices:**
- Be honest about the time commitment
- Both parties should feel the exchange is fair
- If one party completes, the other should honor their commitment
- Document what was agreed (in chat messages)

**Unfair swaps:**
- One person offers an hour of service; the other offers a minute
- One person completes their service; the other ghosts
- Misrepresenting the value or effort required
- Taking advantage of vulnerable or inexperienced users

### 12.2 Cash Transactions
**Important reminder:** The current app does **not process payments**. Cash is exchanged peer-to-peer.

**Best practices:**
- Agree on pricing upfront
- Use secure payment methods (bank transfer, e-payment, cash in person)
- Never prepay large amounts to strangers
- Meet in safe public locations for cash exchanges
- Get a receipt or confirmation if applicable
- If you pay, keep proof (transaction screenshot, receipt)

**Red flags:**
- Requests for payment outside normal channels
- Pressure to pay immediately or lose the deal
- Requests to pay via gift cards or cryptocurrency
- Payment requests for "deposits" or "guarantees"
