## 4. SKILL SWAPS vs. CASH TRANSACTIONS

### 4.1 Skill Swaps (Barter Exchange)
Skill swaps are peer-to-peer exchanges of services without monetary payment.

- Both parties agree to exchange their respective skills
- No payment is processed through the App
- Disputes are resolved through mediation (see Section 8)
- The App facilitates the connection; it does not guarantee completion or quality

### 4.2 Cash Transactions
Cash transactions involve direct payment from a client to a provider.

**Important:** The App is currently a **peer-to-peer platform** and does NOT process payments directly:
- Providers and clients negotiate payment terms in the chat
- Payment is exchanged directly (cash, bank transfer, mobile wallet) outside the App
- The Company **does not escrow, hold, or mediate payments**
- Disputes over payment are between the parties; the Company provides dispute reporting only

**Future Enhancement:** A secure in-app payment system (via Stripe, PayMongo, or similar) will be added in a later release. This document will be updated accordingly.

### 4.3 Provider Pricing
Providers may set their own rates. The Company:
- Does not take a commission on cash transactions (current version)
- Does not validate pricing fairness
- Reserves the right to implement a commission structure in future versions
- Does not guarantee payment or provide refund processing

## 6. NO-SHOWS, CANCELLATIONS & REFUNDS

### 6.1 Cancellation Policy
Clients and providers may cancel bookings using the in-app feature:
- **Approved Bookings:** Can be cancelled up to 24 hours before the scheduled time
- **Cancellations within 24 hours:** May incur a "no-show" mark on the canceling party's record
- **After the scheduled time:** Cannot be cancelled; must proceed to completion or dispute

### 6.2 No-Show Definition
A "no-show" occurs when:
- A scheduled session is not completed as agreed
- One party fails to appear without notice or acceptable reason
- A provider fails to upload proof of work after the scheduled time

### 6.3 No-Show Reporting
The non-offending party may report a no-show through the App, which triggers a review by an administrator. The reporting party should include:
- Date and time of the scheduled session
- Location details
- Evidence (messages, screenshots, etc.)

### 6.4 Consequences of No-Shows
Users with multiple no-show reports may face:
- Account warnings
- Profile suspension or deactivation
- Removal of verified status
- Permanent account termination (three-strike system: **3 substantiated no-shows = account suspension**)

### 6.5 Refunds
In peer-to-peer cash transactions:
- The Company does not process refunds
- Disputes over payment are between the parties
- Admins may review disputes but cannot force refunds
- Users are encouraged to resolve disputes amicably
